
# BioNexa AI Practice

## Purpose

Nexa Practice uses the existing BioNexa AI service to generate original study questions and flashcards.

## AI-powered features

- Generate multiple-choice quizzes from a chosen topic.
- Choose beginner, intermediate or advanced difficulty.
- Generate 5, 10 or 15 questions.
- Receive feedback and explanations after answering.
- Generate 10–30 flashcards.
- Save generated flashcard decks locally.
- Record quiz scores and completed topics.
- Identify topics with lower recorded average scores.

## Internet requirements

A working internet connection is required to generate new questions and flashcards.

Previously saved flashcard decks and practice history are stored locally on the device.

## Educational accuracy

AI-generated questions are practice materials, not official examination questions.

Students should compare important facts, calculations and medical or engineering guidance with course notes, lecturers and trusted academic references.

## Data storage

Practice history and saved flashcard decks are stored in the browser's local storage using the BioNexa storage service.

Clearing the app's local data may remove these records.
